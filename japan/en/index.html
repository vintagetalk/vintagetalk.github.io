How to buy

You can bring in a whole container, buy as many bales as you need from our warehouse, or pick out just the clothes you want with bale picking.

Buy a container

We import a whole container from Japan and deliver it to your warehouse. The "One container" price in the list is for delivery to our warehouse in Yangju, Gyeonggi-do; for other destinations only the trucking is added.

  1. Choose and askTell us the type you want and where to deliver.
  2. Set the shipping dateEach type has fixed shipping dates every month.
  3. Contract and paymentThe amount is set at that day's exchange rate, and we issue a tax invoice.
  4. About 2 weeks after shippingWe clear customs when it arrives at Incheon.
  5. DeliveryPrices include delivery to our warehouse in Yangju, Gyeonggi-do. For other destinations we quote the trucking separately.

Buy bales

You buy as many bales as you need from stock at our warehouse in Yangju, Gyeonggi-do. A small warehouse storage charge is added compared with buying a whole container.

  1. Check warehouse stockWe will tell you the types and quantities in stock by KakaoTalk or phone.
  2. Choose quantity, get a quoteTell us the type and how many bales, and we will quote the per-bale price in the list plus a small storage charge.
  3. Loading at the warehouseWe load your truck with a forklift. A bale weighs about 370 kg, so please come with a truck.

Bale picking Open a bale and take only what you want

No need to buy a whole bale. We open a bale at the warehouse and you pick only what suits your store, so a picking fee is charged separately from container and bale prices.

  1. BookBook a day by KakaoTalk or phone, and we will tell you which bales are in and what the bale picking costs.
  2. Pay the picking feeThe fee depends on the bale and is set when each shipment arrives.
  3. Take about 10%From one bale you take up to about 10% of its weight. A 370 kg bale works out to 37 kg, which we round up to 40 kg.
  4. Taking moreAnything over the set weight is charged per extra kg.

How we choose

VINTAGETALK imports Japanese used clothing and sells it in its own online shop, . We work every type ourselves and only offer the ones that stand out on price, quality and value.

Unpicked

General used clothing is shipped as collected, without brand items picked out.

Collected indoors

Because of the snow, Hokkaido clothing is all collected indoors and mostly handed over in person, so there is little waste.

Washed clothing only

Very dirty, wet or smelly clothing is refused at collection.

Clothing-focused containers only

We do not handle containers full of goods that are hard to clear through Korean customs.

FAQ

Why do prices change every day?

We pay for the clothing in yen. Every morning we recalculate at the KEB Hana Bank transfer rate, so prices rise and fall with the yen.

Can I get a tax invoice?

Yes. Prices shown include VAT, and we issue a Korean tax invoice.

When will I receive my order?

A container usually reaches Incheon about 2 weeks after leaving Japan, sometimes later because of weather or port congestion. Bales in our warehouse can be picked up right away.

What about types marked "Ask for a quote"?

We check the current price and shipping schedule in Japan and let you know. Some types are priced by tender.

Can I buy just part of a bale?

Yes, with bale picking. On your booked day you pay the picking fee, we open one bale, and you take up to about 10% of its arrival weight. A 370 kg bale works out to 37 kg, which we round up to 40 kg; anything more is charged per extra kg. The fee and per-kg charge are set when each shipment arrives.

Can I ask in my own language?

Yes. Write to us in any language by KakaoTalk, WhatsApp or email. We translate your message and reply in the same language.

Can I see more photos?

Message us on KakaoTalk, WhatsApp or email and we will send the photos and videos we have for each type.
